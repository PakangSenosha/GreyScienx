# GreyScienx research standards sources

`prose-corpus.yaml` is the versioned reference manifest for expository surface tone. It stores links,
selection reasons and measurable constraints without copying the external articles.

The prose corpus does not replace the mathematical-writing standard, the semantic-twin schema,
source verification or human editing. A reference article is never evidence for a GreyScienx claim
unless it is separately registered and cited as a research source.
