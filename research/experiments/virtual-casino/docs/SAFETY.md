# Safety and responsible presentation review

This release is a statistical observatory, not a casino.

- All currency is synthetic, non-transferable, non-purchasable, and non-redeemable.
- There are no deposits, withdrawals, prizes, coupons, tokens, crypto-assets, player accounts, or payment integrations.
- Visitors cannot place, choose, time, or alter a wager. The application has no public write endpoint.
- There are no “bet”, “spin”, “deal”, or “play now” controls, leaderboards for visitor competition, celebratory effects, autoplay audio, countdowns, near-miss treatments, affiliate links, operator advertising, or bonus offers.
- Winner tables always appear with the full distribution and across-world policy context.
- Copy uses “lowest loss” or observed metric descriptions, never a “best strategy” claim.
- Synthetic status and no-cash-value language appears on every experiment view.
- Charts have text summaries, table alternatives, labelled axes, and colour-independent encodings. Motion is nonessential and disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- The exhibit is not marketed to minors and uses no child-oriented mascots or reward system.

Synthetic policies are not people and cannot diagnose or estimate gambling disorder. Any future visitor research or human data collection requires a separate consent, privacy, and ethics protocol.

For South African readers, the public safety view links to the South African Responsible Gambling Foundation and its counselling line. The resource is presented as support, not as an operator endorsement.
